# Alterations and Repair

> We offer full service alterations for all sorts of clothes and materials, old or new. We will give your clothes that instance recognisable elegance, that only comes from a great-fitting garment, tailored to your body and style.
>
> The perfect fit is our guarantee. We like to assess each alteration on the customer to ensure the finish is perfect, before they leave the shop.
>
> We are alteration specialists and can tailor your suits, jackets, coats, dresses, trousers, skirts, waistcoats, and more. Why trust a local dry cleaner to perform your alterations, when you can utilise professional tailors, who are exclusively focused on altering garments? Then come to Arta Bespoke Tailoring.
